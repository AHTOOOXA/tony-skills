# motion-video — house rules (example)

Put yours at `~/.claude/motion-video/house-rules.md`; the skill reads it first. Keep it SHORT: only things
that actually went wrong for you, each with your own words from the moment (the quote is the why).
Long rule lists make the agent cautious and the films bland — leave taste free.

- Real UI only — "why are some screens fake? ruins it"
- App footage fullscreen, no phone frames
- Real fonts and logos
- Give me a finished file, plus variants when it's a taste call

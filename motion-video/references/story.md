# Story, hook, copy, characters

## Concept

See `concept.md`: the claim, the tension, idea generators, how to pick, and the tests that catch a
brandbook/feature tour. In short: one claim, proven by one picture, carried by one spine.

## Hook and structure

- Hook in the first 2 s, the product inside 3 s (TikTok: proposition in 3 s; hook within 6).
- Frame 0 is postable: settled, readable, not a fade from black.
- Shape for 15–25 s (brag): hook 2–3 s → reveal 2–4 s → 2–3 moments of the product doing its job
  (entry → action → result, a visible finger/cursor) → payoff/end card 2–4 s.
- Many feeds start muted (41 % of Meta video ads meant nothing without sound): make the picture carry the story too.
- One idea per short. If the source is long, pick one angle — don't compress.
- The end card: name + where to get it, held to the last frame. Rhyme the last frame with the first.

## Copy

Specific beats generic. The brand's own lines (tagline, manifesto, product names) are gold — use them big
and with confidence. A real phrase from the viewer's life is a strong hook. What reads weak is copy that
could be anyone's ("Innovative AI assistant", "Unlock your potential"), and explaining what the picture
already shows. Short lines, set big, timed to the music.
No numbers on screen without a source; label illustrative data as such.

## Reference-driven style

Naming a style beats describing it. Take a reference film: frames every 0.5 s (`ffmpeg -vf fps=2`), write
`style_guide.md` (palette hex, type family/weight/tracking, shot lengths, transitions, camera, grain, how text
enters/exits) and `shotlist.md`. Take the grammar, never the content.

## Characters and mascots

- Code draws geometric characters well and organic humans badly (a hooded figure from three blobs looked
  "like MS Paint"). If the brand has drawn art, use it as cut-out parts on a rig instead of redrawing.
- Build a character bible first: proportions, palette sampled from a sheet, 8–10 expressions, an identity lock.
  Iterate the model sheet as stills until it's right, then animate.
- Acting: anticipation → take with overshoot → settle; eyes lead, the body follows, tails drag; no twinning
  (code copies values — both ears end up identical); exaggerate (subtle reads as nothing); show the thought
  (notice → think → act). A mascot that only bobs is a sticker.
- The mascot may earn one still close-up as the rhythm break (onetake: push in, hold silent ~1 s, blink,
  pupils snap to the next thing, the camera follows the gaze out).
- Medium shot: the character ~25–40 % of frame height; keep its face out of the caption band.
